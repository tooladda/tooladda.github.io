    What Makes a Custom Alias Valid

    An alias is the part after the slash. The tool checks it before anything is sent: 5 to 30 characters, using only letters A–Z, digits and the underscore. Each example below was run through that same check.

    AliasResultWhy
    my_brandAccepted8 characters of letters, numbers and underscores.
    Spring_Sale_2026Accepted16 characters of letters, numbers and underscores.
    my-brandRefusedA hyphen is not allowed; use an underscore.
    saleRefusedFour characters; the minimum is five.
    summer saleRefusedSpaces are not allowed.
    café_menuRefusedOnly plain letters: é is refused.
    launch_2026_newsletter_octoberAccepted30 characters of letters, numbers and underscores.
    launch_2026_newsletter_october_RefusedThirty-one characters; the maximum is thirty.

    Leave the alias empty and the service picks a short code for you. Either way, the link comes from the first free service that answers, tried in this order:

    1. da.gd
    2. is.gd
    3. v.gd
    4. TinyURL without an alias only

    So an alias can come back as da.gd/your_alias rather than is.gd/…, and if the name is already taken on the service that answers, you will see its message and can try another. The long address needs a real domain with a dot in it; https:// is added if you leave it off, and localhost links are refused.

    The Complete Guide to URL Shortening

    What Is a URL Shortener?

    A URL shortener takes a long web address and creates a compact redirect link that sends visitors to the same destination. Instead of sharing a messy link with tracking parameters, session IDs, and deep folder paths, you share something short and clean that's easier to read, type, and remember.

    Why Shorten URLs?

    • Cleaner sharing: Short links look professional in social posts, bios, and printed materials.
    • Character limits: Platforms like Twitter/X and SMS reward shorter URLs — every character counts.
    • Easier to remember: With a custom alias, your link can include your brand name instead of random characters.
    • QR-friendly: Shorter URLs produce simpler QR codes that scan more reliably at small sizes.
    • Less breakage: Long URLs often wrap awkwardly in email clients and chat apps, breaking the link when copied.

    How URL Shortening Works

    When you shorten a link, the service stores a mapping between a short code (like abc123) and your original long URL. When someone clicks the short link, the service looks up the mapping and redirects the browser to the destination. ToolAdda connects to trusted free shortening APIs to create that mapping for you — no account needed on your end.

    Custom Aliases Explained

    A custom alias (also called a custom slug or branded link) lets you choose the ending of your short URL — for example, is.gd/my-brand instead of is.gd/xK9p2. Aliases must be unique: if someone else already claimed a name, you'll need to pick a different one. Use letters, numbers, and underscores; keep it between 5 and 30 characters.

    URL Shortener vs Link-in-Bio Tools

    A URL shortener creates a single compact redirect for one destination. Link-in-bio tools (like Linktree) host a landing page with multiple links. Use a shortener when you have one URL to share; use a link-in-bio page when you need a menu of several destinations from one profile link.

    Best Practices for Short Links

    • Never shorten URLs that contain passwords, API keys, private tokens, or personal data in query strings.
    • For important business links (invoices, contracts, checkout), consider a paid shortener with uptime guarantees and analytics.
    • Pair campaign links with our UTM Builder first, then shorten the tagged URL so you can track traffic in Google Analytics.
    • Test the short link after creating it — open it in a private window to confirm it reaches the right page.
    • Use custom aliases for links you'll share repeatedly (social bios, email signatures, print materials).

    Short Links for Social Media

    Instagram bios, TikTok descriptions, LinkedIn posts, and Twitter/X threads all benefit from shorter URLs. A compact link leaves more room for your caption and looks more trustworthy than a long string of parameters. Combine shortening with UTM tags to measure which posts drive the most traffic.

    Short Links for Print & QR Codes

    Flyers, business cards, restaurant menus, and conference badges work best with short URLs or QR codes. Every link shortened on ToolAdda includes a downloadable QR code — print it directly or embed it in your design. Shorter underlying URLs produce cleaner QR patterns that scan faster from a distance.

    Do Short Links Expire?

    Free shortening services generally keep links active indefinitely, but there's no universal guarantee. Links may stop working if the service changes policies, the alias violates terms, or the destination URL itself is removed. For mission-critical links, use a provider with a documented uptime SLA and link management dashboard.
