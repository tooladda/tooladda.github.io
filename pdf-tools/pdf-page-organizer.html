The details

What a PDF page organizer actually does

A PDF is an ordered list of page objects. When you rearrange, delete or duplicate pages, nothing inside a page has to be re-encoded — the file simply gets rebuilt with the pages referenced in a different order. That is exactly what this tool does: it reads the page objects out of your document, lets you shuffle them on screen, and writes a new PDF that points at them in the order you chose. Text stays selectable, images keep their resolution, and the file size stays close to the original.

Why use this one instead of a desktop editor

Most page organizers either upload your file to a server, cap you at a couple of documents a day, or hide reordering behind a subscription. This one runs entirely inside the browser tab, so your document never leaves the device — no account, no daily limit, no watermark, and no waiting on an upload progress bar. You get a proper editing surface too: live thumbnails, multi-page selection, bulk rotate, duplicate, move-to-start and move-to-end, undo and redo, and the ability to merge pages from several files into one grid.

Merge pages from several PDFs

Click Add PDF after loading the first document and the new file's pages join the same grid — think of it as merging PDFs with the page order under your control. That makes it easy to interleave scans — for example, putting the second page of a double-sided scan between the pages of the first pass, or dropping a signed page back into the middle of a contract. Every page keeps a reference to the document it came from, so quality is untouched no matter how many files you combine.

Common use cases

People organize PDF pages to fix the order of a scanned document fed through the feeder backwards, to remove blank or duplicate scan pages, to straighten sideways ones (the same job the dedicated Rotate PDF tool does), to move an annexure to the end of a report, to build a submission pack from several files, to duplicate a form page so a family can each fill one in, to pull a section out and split it into its own document, and to reorder slides exported from a presentation. Students reorganise lecture notes; offices assemble tender documents; anyone with a phone scanner uses it to tidy up a batch before sending it on.

Undo, redo and safety

Editing a long document is much less nerve-racking when you can step back. Every action here — a drag, a delete, a duplicate, a rotation, a bulk move — goes onto an undo stack, and Ctrl+Z or the Undo button takes you back one step at a time. Nothing is written to your original file at any point: the PDF on your disk is only read, and a brand-new file is produced when you click save.

Reordering, splitting or merging — which do you need?

The three overlap enough to be worth separating. Merging joins whole documents end to end, in the order you list them — right when you have five files and want one. Splitting divides one document by a rule: every page, a range, every N pages, odd and even. Organizing, this tool, is for the cases neither covers: the order is nearly right but two pages are swapped, page 7 is a blank scan, the annexure belongs at the end, and the signed page from a second file has to slot into the middle. If you can describe what you want as a rule, use Split; if you have to look at the pages to decide, organize them here.

How many pages can it handle?

The page limit is your device, not the tool. Thumbnails render one at a time and only when they scroll into view, and each rendered page is cached, so a 300-page document stays responsive after the first screen. Rebuilding the file at the end is the slower step — the pages are copied, not re-encoded, so it scales with page count rather than file size, and a few hundred pages take a couple of seconds on a normal laptop. The 100 MB ceiling is per file, and you can load several files.

Privacy & security

The document is opened and rebuilt with JavaScript in your own browser, so it is never uploaded, logged or stored anywhere. The page is served over HTTPS, requires no account, and keeps nothing after you close the tab. For confidential contracts, medical records, ID documents or financial statements that means the file stays entirely under your control — which is not something a server-side organizer can promise.

Works on mobile

On a phone the thumbnail grid reflows to fit the screen and each page carries its own arrow, rotate, duplicate and delete buttons, so you never need drag and drop. It is the quickest way to reorder a document you just scanned with your phone camera before emailing it — no app install, no sign-up.

Frequently asked questions

How do I reorder the pages of a PDF?

Upload the file, then drag any thumbnail to a new position — or use the ← and → buttons on each page. Click Save organized PDF when the order looks right.

Can I delete pages for free?

Yes. Use the ✕ button on a page, or tick several pages and choose Delete selected. No limits, no watermark.

Can I combine pages from two PDFs?

Yes. Click Add PDF and the new document's pages join the same grid so you can interleave them freely.

Is my file uploaded anywhere?

No. Everything runs in your browser; the PDF is never sent to a server.

Can I undo a mistake?

Yes — every change is undoable with the Undo button or Ctrl+Z, and redoable with Ctrl+Y.

Does rearranging reduce quality?

No. Pages are copied as-is with no re-compression, so text and images are untouched.

Can I extract just a few pages?

Yes. Tick the pages you want and click Export selected pages to get them as a separate PDF.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes. The grid is responsive and every page has buttons, so drag and drop is optional.

Can I duplicate a page?

Yes. The ⧉ button inserts an identical copy right after the page.

Is there a size limit?

Files up to 100 MB work. Large scans just take a little longer to preview.

Can I rotate while reordering?

Yes. Rotate a single page with ⟳, or select several and rotate the whole batch.

What about bookmarks and form fields?

Page content is preserved exactly. Document-level extras such as bookmarks and form actions are not carried into the rebuilt file.

Do I need Adobe Acrobat?

No. Nothing to install — this does the same job free in your browser.

Can I organize a password-protected PDF?

Unlock it first with the PDF Password Remover, then load it here.

Will the result open everywhere?

Yes. It is a standard PDF and opens in every modern reader.
