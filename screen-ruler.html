    How it works

    The on-screen ruler tracks pixel width and height as you drag and resize it. For images, each click sets a point in the image's native coordinate space, so distance, angle, and area stay accurate at any zoom level. Calibration maps pixels to real units using a reference length you provide.

    Frequently asked questions

    How do I measure distance on an image?

    Open the "Measure image" tab, upload or paste an image, keep the Distance tool selected, then click a start point and an end point. The distance appears instantly in pixels (and real units once calibrated).

    How do I convert pixels to mm, cm, or inches?

    Measure a length you already know with the Distance tool, type that length and pick its unit in the calibration box, then press "Set from last distance". Every measurement then shows both pixels and real units.

    Can I measure angles and areas?

    Yes. The Angle tool measures the angle between three points (the middle click is the vertex). The Area tool draws a rectangle and reports its width, height, area, and perimeter.

    Can I convert pixels to real-world units like mm or inches?

    Yes. Measure a known length on the image, enter that length and its unit, and press Set as reference. Every measurement is then shown in both pixels and your chosen unit.

    Can I paste a screenshot directly?

    Yes. Copy any image or take a screenshot, then press Ctrl+V (Cmd+V on Mac) while on the Measure image tab and it loads instantly.

    Are my images uploaded anywhere?

    No. All processing is local to your browser, so your images never leave your device.

    Does the on-screen ruler measure real screen pixels?

    It measures CSS pixels within the page. On high-DPI displays one CSS pixel can map to several physical pixels, so use image calibration when you need exact real-world sizing.
