About car loans

A car loan helps you finance a new or used vehicle by spreading the cost over monthly EMIs. The loan amount is typically the car price minus your down payment.

Key highlights

1–7 year tenure • 10–20% down payment • Rates from 7–15% p.a. • Fixed EMI reducing balance

EMI = [P × R × (1+R)^N] / [(1+R)^N − 1]

P = loan amount, R = monthly interest rate, N = tenure in months. Interest is calculated on reducing balance.

Frequently asked questions

How is car loan EMI calculated in India?

Car loan EMI uses the reducing balance method: EMI = [P × R × (1+R)^N] / [(1+R)^N − 1], where P is the loan amount (car price minus down payment), R is the monthly interest rate, and N is the number of months.

What is the typical down payment for a car loan in India?

Most banks and NBFCs require a minimum down payment of 10–20% of the car's on-road price. A higher down payment reduces your loan amount and monthly EMI.

What is the interest rate for car loans in India?

Car loan interest rates typically range from 7% to 15% per annum depending on the lender, your credit score, vehicle type (new vs used), and loan tenure.

What is the maximum tenure for a car loan in India?

Most lenders offer car loan tenures between 1 and 7 years. New car loans can go up to 7 years, while used car loans are usually capped at 5 years.

Can I prepay my car loan early?

Yes. Most banks allow partial or full prepayment. Some lenders may charge a prepayment penalty (typically 2–5%) if paid within the first 12 months. Prepaying reduces total interest.
