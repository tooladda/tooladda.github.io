What is the Image Background Remover?

The ToolAdda image background remover is a free tool that erases a photo's background and lets you replace it with transparency, a solid color, or a new image — without an AI model, an account, or a single byte leaving your browser. It works by sampling the colors around the edges of your photo and flood-filling inward, similar to a "magic wand" tool, so it works especially well on product shots, ID photos, logos, and any picture taken against a plain wall or screen.

How to remove or change a background

4 easy steps

  1. Upload a photo by dragging it in or clicking Browse image.
  2. Click Auto-remove background, adjusting tolerance and edge softness as needed.
  3. Switch to the erase or restore brush to clean up any missed spots.
  4. Pick a new background — transparent, solid color, or a custom image — and download the PNG.

Frequently asked questions

How does this background remover work without uploading my photo?

It reads pixels directly in the browser with the Canvas API and flood-fills from the edges inward. Nothing is ever sent to a server.

Does it work with any photo, or only plain backgrounds?

It works best with a fairly plain or solid-colored background. For busy backgrounds, use the erase and restore brushes to refine the result.

Can I change the background instead of just removing it?

Yes — keep it transparent, fill it with any solid color, or place a different image behind your subject.

Why does the auto-remove leave some edges rough?

Raise the tolerance slider if background remains, raise edge softness for smoother anti-aliasing, or use the brushes to touch up manually.

What image formats can I upload and download?

Upload JPG, PNG, WebP, or most common formats. The result always downloads as a transparency-preserving PNG.

Is there a size limit on the image?

Large photos are automatically scaled to a maximum of 2200px on the longest side for fast, smooth processing.
