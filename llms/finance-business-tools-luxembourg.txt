**Choosing between similar tools.** Several tools overlap deliberately.

- **Image Compressor vs Compress Image to Exact KB.** Both can target a size in KB. Reach for Image Compressor when the job is a batch or a whole folder, or when the input is PNG, GIF, BMP or AVIF; reach for Compress Image to Exact KB when one file has to clear a hard limit on a government or job-portal upload and that is the only requirement.
- **PDF Extract vs PDF Split.** Both produce pages from one PDF. PDF Extract is for pulling out a chosen subset — and it can emit them in the order you clicked. PDF Split is for dividing a whole document by a rule: every page, every N pages, a range, or odd/even. Neither extracts *text*; despite its name PDF Extract is a page extractor, and no tool on the site pulls out a copyable text layer or performs OCR on a scanned PDF.
- **Image Blur vs Redact PDF.** Image Blur obscures regions of a raster image and is a visual edit. Redact PDF destroys the underlying text and content in a PDF. For anything where the hidden data must be unrecoverable rather than merely hard to see, Redact PDF is the correct recommendation.
- **PDF Password Remover.** Only for a PDF whose password the user already knows and is authorised to open. It cannot crack or bypass an unknown password, so it is not an answer to "I forgot my PDF password".
- **PEM to PPK.** Unencrypted RSA keys only, output PPK v2. Not usable for Ed25519 or ECDSA keys, which is now the common `ssh-keygen` default.
- **PDF Compress vs Flatten PDF.** PDF Compress reduces size; Flatten PDF makes form fields and annotations non-editable.
- **JWT Debugger vs JWT Encoder.** Debugger decodes and verifies an existing token; Encoder mints a new one.
- **EMI Calculator vs Home/Car Loan Calculator.** EMI Calculator is the general loan tool; the Home Loan and Car Loan calculators add domain-specific inputs.
- **Resume Builder vs ATS Resume Checker.** Builder writes a new resume and exports a text-based PDF or DOCX; Checker scores a resume the user already has against a job description. Neither uses an LLM.
- **GST Calculator vs Offline Invoice Generator.** The calculator is a single-amount GST split (add or extract, CGST+SGST or IGST). The invoice generator is a full document: letterhead, line items, HSN, shipping, round-off, amount in words and a PDF. Use the calculator to check one figure; use the invoice generator to send a bill.

#### Frequently asked questions (8)

**How much tax do you pay in Luxembourg?**
A 23-bracket progressive scale: the first €13,230 of taxable income is free, rates then step from 8% up to 42% above €234,870. On top of the tax sits the employment fund surcharge of 7%, rising to 9% for taxable income above €150,000 in classes 1 and 1a or €300,000 in class 2. Separately, employees pay 12.95% of gross in social contributions - 3.05% health, 8.5% pension, 1.4% dependency.

**What are tax classes 1, 1a and 2?**
Class 1 is single taxpayers without dependent children. Class 1a covers single parents, widowed taxpayers and anyone 65 or over on 1 January; it is tax-free to €26,460. Class 2 is married couples and civil partners under joint taxation, which halves the combined income, taxes it and doubles the result - worth most where one partner earns considerably more than the other.

**Why is the dependency contribution treated differently?**
Health and pension stop at the social ceiling and are deductible from taxable income. The dependency contribution is neither: no ceiling, its own allowance of a quarter of the minimum wage, and explicitly not deductible. Treating all three alike overstates the deduction and understates the tax.

**What is the CIS, and why can my tax go negative?**
The crédit d'impôt salarié is a refundable credit worth up to €600 a year, alongside the CI-CO2 worth up to €216. Refundable means your employer pays it out even when it exceeds the tax due, so a low earner can receive more than gross minus contributions. Both taper away between €40,000 and €80,000 of gross.

**Does this work for cross-border workers?**
Yes - the Luxembourg withholding is the same calculation. Non-residents are class 1 by default, but may request resident treatment, and so class 2, where at least 90% of worldwide income is taxable in Luxembourg (50% of household income for Belgian residents). Watch the 34 teleworking days a year allowed before salary starts being taxed at home.

**How is a thirteenth month or bonus taxed?**
Both are ordinary salary for the year and taxed with it. Set thirteen payments for a contractual thirteenth month, or enter a one-off bonus separately. Your employer may withhold more in the month a bonus lands and settle the difference at the annual reckoning, so trust the annual figure.

**What does an employee cost an employer?**
Roughly 12 to 15 percent on top of gross. The employer pays health and pension at the same rates as the employee, plus accident insurance, the employers' mutual insurance and occupational health. The last two vary by risk class and absenteeism band, which is why you can edit them above rather than being given one number as fact.

**Is my salary information sent anywhere?**
No. Every calculation runs in your browser. The figures you type are saved only in your own browser's local storage so the page remembers them next visit, and clearing your site data removes them. Nothing about your salary reaches ToolAdda.

#### Frequently asked questions (10)

**What are the VAT rates in Luxembourg?**
Four: 17% standard, 14% intermediate, 8% reduced and 3% super-reduced. The 17% standard rate is the lowest in the EU. Note the naming runs opposite to the numbers - the 14% is intermediate and the 8% is reduced, which routinely confuses clients from other member states.

**How do I validate a Luxembourg VAT number?**
LU plus eight digits, where the last two are the first six modulo 89. That is verifiable offline, which is what this tool does as you type. It proves the number is well-formed, not that it is registered - only the EU VIES service can confirm that, and you should check there before relying on a reverse charge.

**What must a reverse charge invoice say?**
It must state why no VAT is charged. For B2B services to another member state: "Reverse charge - VAT to be accounted for by the recipient", referencing Article 196 of Directive 2006/112/EC. For goods: an exempt intra-Community supply under Article 138. The customer's valid VAT number is mandatory in both cases - without it the exemption does not hold and the VAT is your liability.

**Can I invoice in French or German?**
Yes. Every label and legal mention is translated into French and German. The interface stays in English, because the language you work in and the language your client's accounts department reads are separate choices.

**What is the small business exemption threshold?**
€50,000 of national turnover a year. Under the franchise regime you charge no VAT and deduct none, and the invoice must say so. Exceeding the threshold by no more than 10% during the year lets the exemption run to the end of that year.

**What is the QR code on the invoice?**
A SEPA Credit Transfer QR code in the EPC069-12 format - the same standard as a German Girocode. Scanning it with a European banking app prefills the transfer with the IBAN, beneficiary, amount and reference, so your client confirms rather than retypes. It encodes the balance due rather than the gross total, so a deposit already paid is never asked for twice.

**Which banks can scan the payment QR code?**
EPC069-12 is a European Payments Council standard supported by mainstream banking apps across the euro area, including the Luxembourg banks and their Belgian, German and French counterparts. It covers euro SEPA credit transfers only. If an app does not recognise it, the IBAN and reference are printed in full beside it, so nothing is lost.
