**Choosing between similar tools.** Several tools overlap deliberately.

- **Image Compressor vs Compress Image to Exact KB.** Both can target a size in KB. Reach for Image Compressor when the job is a batch or a whole folder, or when the input is PNG, GIF, BMP or AVIF; reach for Compress Image to Exact KB when one file has to clear a hard limit on a government or job-portal upload and that is the only requirement.
- **PDF Extract vs PDF Split.** Both produce pages from one PDF. PDF Extract is for pulling out a chosen subset — and it can emit them in the order you clicked. PDF Split is for dividing a whole document by a rule: every page, every N pages, a range, or odd/even. Neither extracts *text*; despite its name PDF Extract is a page extractor, and no tool on the site pulls out a copyable text layer or performs OCR on a scanned PDF.
- **Image Blur vs Redact PDF.** Image Blur obscures regions of a raster image and is a visual edit. Redact PDF destroys the underlying text and content in a PDF. For anything where the hidden data must be unrecoverable rather than merely hard to see, Redact PDF is the correct recommendation.
- **PDF Password Remover.** Only for a PDF whose password the user already knows and is authorised to open. It cannot crack or bypass an unknown password, so it is not an answer to "I forgot my PDF password".
- **PEM to PPK.** Unencrypted RSA keys only, output PPK v2. Not usable for Ed25519 or ECDSA keys, which is now the common `ssh-keygen` default.
- **PDF Compress vs Flatten PDF.** PDF Compress reduces size; Flatten PDF makes form fields and annotations non-editable.
- **JWT Debugger vs JWT Encoder.** Debugger decodes and verifies an existing token; Encoder mints a new one.
- **EMI Calculator vs Home/Car Loan Calculator.** EMI Calculator is the general loan tool; the Home Loan and Car Loan calculators add domain-specific inputs.
- **Resume Builder vs ATS Resume Checker.** Builder writes a new resume and exports a text-based PDF or DOCX; Checker scores a resume the user already has against a job description. Neither uses an LLM.
- **GST Calculator vs Offline Invoice Generator.** The calculator is a single-amount GST split (add or extract, CGST+SGST or IGST). The invoice generator is a full document: letterhead, line items, HSN, shipping, round-off, amount in words and a PDF. Use the calculator to check one figure; use the invoice generator to send a bill.

Yes. Employee EPF is calculated at 12% of Basic plus DA, with an optional statutory wage ceiling of ₹15,000 per month that most employers apply. **How is professional tax calculated?**
Professional tax is a state levy under Article 276 of the Constitution, capped at ₹2,500 per person per year. Select the state where the employee works and the tool applies that state's slab to the gross salary. Some states - Delhi, Haryana, Uttar Pradesh, Rajasthan, Punjab and others - do not levy it on salary at all, so choose "No professional tax" for those. Because states revise slabs in their annual finance acts, the figure is always editable and every slab table on this page carries a last-reviewed date.

**Why doesn't the tool calculate TDS automatically?**
Because it honestly cannot, and a tool that pretended otherwise would produce wrong payslips. Monthly TDS under Section 192 is not a function of one month's salary. It depends on the projected income for the whole financial year, the employee's regime choice, their Chapter VI-A declarations, any house property loss, income and TDS from a previous employer, and how much has already been deducted in earlier months - with the balance re-spread over the months remaining. We keep TDS as a manual entry that you copy from your payroll computation. We would rather say so than fake a number that lands on a legal document.

**Is a computer-generated payslip valid?**
Yes. Indian law prescribes what a wage record must contain, not that it must be handwritten or wet-signed. A digitally generated payslip issued by the employer is routinely accepted by banks, landlords and visa authorities. It is common practice to print the line "This is a computer-generated payslip and does not require a signature", which this tool adds by default. If your internal policy or a specific counterparty requires a signature, upload a signature image or leave the block empty for a manual sign.

**Is it legally mandatory to give employees a salary slip in India?**
For most employers, yes. Section 13A of the Payment of Wages Act, 1936 requires wage and deduction registers; Rule 26(2) of the Minimum Wages (Central) Rules, 1950 requires wage slips at least one day before disbursement; the Code on Wages, 2019 consolidates these obligations and requires wage slips in the prescribed form; and state Shops and Establishments Acts impose their own duties. Which rule binds you depends on your state, establishment type and headcount, so confirm the position with a labour law advisor.

**What should a valid salary slip contain?**
At minimum: employer name and address, employee name and identifier, the wage period, days worked or paid, each wage component listed separately, each deduction listed separately with its reason, gross wages, total deductions and net payable. In Indian practice also carry designation, date of joining, PAN, UAN or PF number, ESI number where applicable, the bank account credited and the payment date. This generator includes every one of those fields.

**Can I use this for contractors, consultants or part-time staff?**
Be careful, because it is a genuine compliance trap. A payslip is a document of employment. If someone is engaged on a contract for services and invoices you, issuing them a payslip with PF and professional tax lines is one of the facts a labour authority or tax officer will use to argue they were really an employee - which brings PF, ESI, gratuity and TDS exposure, backdated. For a genuine consultant, accept their invoice and pay a professional fee instead. For part-time and fixed-term staff who really are employees, a payslip is correct and this tool works exactly as it does for full-time staff.

**What is the difference between gross salary and net salary?**
Gross salary is the total of everything you earn in the month before anything is taken out - Basic, DA, HRA, conveyance, allowances, bonus and overtime. Net salary, also called take-home, is what is actually credited after employee deductions such as EPF, ESI, professional tax, TDS and any advances. Gross minus total deductions equals net.

**How do I convert CTC into a monthly salary breakup?**
Divide annual CTC by twelve, then subtract the employer's own contributions - employer EPF, employer ESI where applicable, and any gratuity provision - because those are part of CTC but were never yours to take home. What remains is gross salary. Basic is normally 40-50% of CTC, HRA is 50% of Basic in Delhi, Mumbai, Kolkata and Chennai or 40% elsewhere, and the balance becomes special allowance. The CTC breakup mode in this tool does all of that in one click, then subtracts employee EPF, ESI and professional tax to show your real in-hand figure.

**Does it work on mobile?**
Yes. The form is built mobile-first with numeric keypads on money fields, a sticky Preview button that opens the payslip full screen, and a sticky Download bar. The PDF is generated at true A4 dimensions regardless of screen size, so a payslip made on a phone prints identically to one made on a desktop.

**Can I use it for a company outside India?**
Yes, with a caveat. Switch off the statutory helpers, choose your currency and date format, rename the rows to your local components, and it becomes a clean generic payslip builder. What it will not do is apply another country's statutory rules - for UK PAYE and National Insurance, US federal and state withholding, or UAE WPS formatting, treat the output as a formatting aid and take the figures from your local payroll system.

**Can I use this payslip for a loan or visa application?**
A payslip issued by your employer that reflects the salary you were actually paid can be submitted anywhere payslips are accepted, whether it was produced by this tool, by SAP or by a spreadsheet. What matters is that the employer issued it and the figures are true. Creating a payslip for yourself showing salary you were not actually paid, or inflating figures on a real one, is a fabricated document. Submitting it to a bank, an embassy or a landlord is misrepresentation and, depending on the circumstances, can amount to forgery and cheating under Indian criminal law - with the loan recalled and the visa refused as the smaller consequences. This tool exists to help employers issue accurate payslips. Please do not use it for anything else.

**Does the payslip prorate automatically for loss of pay?**
Yes. Enter total working days and LOP days, and every earning marked as prorated is scaled by paid days ÷ working days. Fixed reimbursements can be excluded from proration with a single toggle. Rounding is handled so the printed rows always add up exactly to the printed gross - the mismatch most spreadsheet templates get wrong.

**Is a digital or computer-generated rent receipt valid?**
In general yes. What matters legally is that the receipt is genuine, accurate and issued by the person who received the money - not whether it was handwritten. Some processes still expect a signature or a physical stamp, which is why this tool supports both. If a specific authority has told you it needs a wet signature, follow that instruction.

**Does a rent receipt need a signature?**
A receipt is much stronger with the landlord's signature, because that is what turns a statement into an acknowledgement of receipt. For cash payments it is effectively essential. The tool lets the landlord draw, type or upload a signature, and you can leave the block blank to sign on paper.

**Does a rent receipt need a revenue stamp?**
This is an India-specific convention. Indian practice is to affix a ₹1 revenue stamp when a single payment is made in cash above a threshold commonly applied at ₹5,000, with the landlord signing across the stamp. It comes from the receipt article of the Indian Stamp Act, 1899 and its state amendments, so the threshold and stamp value vary by state. It does not apply to bank transfers, UPI or cheques, and has no equivalent in the other countries supported here. Verify the current position for your state.

**Is landlord PAN mandatory to claim HRA in India?**
Where rent paid to one landlord exceeds ₹1,00,000 in a financial year, the standard requirement is to report the landlord's PAN to your employer in your Form 12BB declaration. If the landlord genuinely has no PAN, the long-standing practice has been to obtain a declaration instead - but employers apply this inconsistently and it invites scrutiny. The tool warns you as soon as your rent crosses the threshold without a PAN.

**Can I claim HRA if I pay rent to my parents?**
Yes, if it is genuine. The rent must actually be paid, ideally by bank transfer; your parents must declare it as income in their own return; and you should hold a rent agreement and receipts. What does not work is a paper arrangement with no money moving, or paying rent for a property you already co-own. This is one of the most scrutinised HRA claims there is.

**What if my landlord refuses to give a receipt?**
First check whether they are obliged to - in Ontario they are, on request and free of charge; in the UK a rent book is required for weekly tenancies; several Australian states and US states require a receipt for cash. If they still refuse, pay by a traceable method with the period in the reference line and keep your bank statements. Do not create a receipt yourself and present it as one your landlord issued.

**Is a landlord legally required to provide a rent receipt?**
It depends entirely on where you are; there is no global rule. Ontario: yes, on request. UK: a rent book for weekly periodic tenancies. Australia: generally yes unless the payment method already creates a record. USA: varies by state. India: no general duty on the landlord, but receipts are what your employer needs for HRA. The country note inside the tool states the position for the country you select.

**Can I use a rent receipt as proof of address?**
Sometimes, but it is usually the weakest option, because it is issued by a private individual rather than an institution. A tenancy agreement, utility bill, bank statement or government letter is normally accepted more readily. Where a receipt is accepted it is generally alongside the tenancy agreement rather than instead of it.

**What is the difference between a rent receipt and a rent agreement?**
The agreement is the contract, signed once at the start, setting out parties, property, rent, deposit and term. The receipt is evidence of performance, issued each time rent is paid. You need the agreement to show what was owed and the receipts to show it was paid; neither replaces the other.

**Can I add a security deposit to the receipt?**
Yes, and the tool deliberately puts it on its own line rather than adding it into the rent. A deposit is refundable and is not rent, so folding it in inflates the rent you appear to have paid - exactly the discrepancy that triggers a query on a tax claim. Utilities and late fees are handled the same way.

**How do I show rent paid in cash?**
Select Cash as the payment method. The receipt then becomes your primary evidence, since no bank record exists, which makes the landlord's signature considerably more important and triggers India's revenue stamp convention. Where you have a choice, a bank transfer with the period in the reference line is far stronger evidence than any cash receipt.

**Can I use this if I pay rent weekly?**
Yes. Weekly and fortnightly frequencies are fully supported, which matters in the UK and Australia where weekly rent is normal and most receipt tools assume a monthly cycle. Bulk generation understands them, so you can produce a year of weekly receipts with sequential numbering in one action.

**Can landlords use this to issue receipts to tenants?**
Yes - landlords and property managers are a primary audience. Add a logo, save profiles for multiple properties, set a receipt-number prefix per property, issue a whole year in one action, and export the data as CSV for your accountant or rent ledger.

**Does it work on mobile?**
Yes. The form is mobile-first with numeric keypads on money fields, a sticky Preview button that opens the receipt full screen, and a sticky Download bar. The PDF is produced at true A4 dimensions regardless of screen size.

**Can I edit a receipt after downloading it?**
Not the PDF itself - deliberately, since an easily editable receipt is worth little as evidence. Instead, come back, change the details and download a fresh copy; with "remember my details" ticked the form reloads filled in. If a receipt has already been given to someone, issue a clearly marked corrected receipt rather than quietly replacing it.

**Can I create a receipt for rent I have not actually paid?**
No. A receipt states that money was received on a given date. Creating one for rent that was never paid - to claim a tax exemption, support an application, or satisfy a lender - is document fraud, and where it reduces tax it is tax evasion. Tax authorities cross-verify. In India the landlord PAN you report is matched against that landlord's own return, and claimed rent that never appears as their income is a straightforward mismatch to find. ### ATS Resume Checker

Upload a PDF or DOCX, or paste plain text, and score it against a pasted job description. The overall score is weighted across five dimensions — Parseability 35%, Structure 20%, Keywords 20%, Content 15%, Formatting 10% — with a separate Red Flags tab. Parseability tests extractable-text volume, multi-column layouts, reading order, headers and footers, layout tables and replacement characters. Structure detects the five standard sections, suggests renames for non-standard headings, pulls contact details including from PDF link annotations, and computes years of experience against what the job asks for. Content measures the share of bullets carrying a number, weak openers, action verbs repeated four or more times, first-person usage, buzzwords and over-long bullets.
