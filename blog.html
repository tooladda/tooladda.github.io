01Photo, signature and the exact file-size rules

Filling in a government or job application form

Every Indian exam portal sets its own rules: the photograph must be a certain pixel size, the signature has to be a separate file, and both must come in under a hard limit in kilobytes. This is where most people get stuck. Start with Passport Size Photo Maker, which crops to standard document sizes. Prepare the signature separately in Signature Resizer. Then deal with the file size — if the form says "under 20 KB", use Compress Image to Exact KB, which targets a hard limit; if you are compressing a whole folder, Image Compressor is the better fit. Photo taken on a phone and saved as .HEIC that the portal refuses? HEIC to JPG/PNG Converter turns it into a JPG.

Forms often want the photo and signature inside a single PDF. Use Images to PDF first, then PDF Compress if the result is too large.

02From the question paper to the seating plan

Running an exam or a classroom

Most of the effort in conducting an exam goes into paperwork rather than teaching. Paper Generator prints ruled, graph and dot-grid sheets; Worksheet Generator produces maths and handwriting practice, and Handwriting Tracing Worksheet makes traceable letter sheets on proper four-line ruling for the youngest classes. Students checking whether they can afford to miss a lecture want Attendance Calculator, which answers the two questions the percentage alone does not: how many more they can skip, and how many they must attend in a row to recover. For a multiple-choice paper, OMR Sheet Generator builds a printable answer sheet with a roll-number bubble grid, including NEET and JEE presets. And the job that used to take an evening by hand — Exam Seating Arrangement Planner — turns roll-number ranges and room sizes into a seating chart where no two students from the same class sit next to each other, along with room-wise attendance lists and a door notice for every hall.

Need to pick someone fairly in class? Random Name Picker has a no-repeat raffle mode and shows live odds.

04Loans, investments and everyday calculators

Working out the money

EMI Calculator handles the general loan case; for a house or a car, Home Loan Calculator and Car Loan Calculator add domain-specific inputs. On the investment side there are SIP Calculator, PPF Calculator, FD Calculator and RD Calculator. All of them display figures in the Indian lakh and crore format, and wherever a statutory rate is applied the page carries a "last reviewed" date and leaves the rate editable — because those rates change by state and by establishment. Four of these are built for the United States rather than India, because the arithmetic genuinely differs. US Mortgage Calculator leads with PITI — the whole payment including property tax, insurance, HOA and PMI — and tells you the month mortgage insurance falls away, which is a date most calculators never mention. US Auto Loan Calculator works the car deal from sticker price to signed contract: the trade-in tax credit your state does or does not give you, negative equity rolled forward from the old loan, and every term from 36 to 84 months side by side. Credit Card Payoff Calculator runs every card you hold under both the debt snowball and the debt avalanche and puts a number on what the easier ordering costs. Student Loan Payoff Calculator does the same for federal and private loans, and flags the refinance offer that lowers your rate and still costs you more because the term got longer.

05Merge, split, compress, protect, redact

Working with PDFs

PDF Tools Hub opens everything in one place for day-to-day work. The common operations are straightforward — PDF Merge, PDF Split, PDF Compress, PDF Rotate and Add Watermark to PDF. Two of them are routinely confused, so to be explicit: PDF Extract pulls out the pages you choose (despite the name it does not extract text), whereas PDF Split divides a whole document by a rule. When the job is not one rule but a hundred small decisions — this page first, that one deleted, this one duplicated, and three pages pulled in from a second file — PDF Page Organizer gives you a thumbnail grid you can drag around, with undo behind every change. And when something hidden has to be genuinely gone rather than merely obscured, you want Redact PDF, because it destroys the underlying text.

Three jobs come up once a document is otherwise finished. Add Page Numbers to PDF stamps a running number in any of six positions, in plain digits, roman numerals, "Page 3 of 24" or a custom pattern such as ABC-{n}, and can skip a cover page or start counting at any number. Crop PDF trims the margins: drag a box on the page, type the four margins in millimetres, or let auto-trim find the white border of a scan — per page, which is what a book scan with a drifting text block needs. And Sign PDF puts your signature on it, drawn with a finger, typed in a handwriting font, or photographed on paper with the background removed, then placed at any size and any angle.

Cropping and redacting are not the same thing. A crop only hides what falls outside the box — the content is still in the file. To remove something for good, use Redact PDF.

06Resize, crop, compress, remove backgrounds

Editing photos and images

Image Tools Hub opens everything in one place, with a table for working out which tool a given job needs. The basics — Image Resizer, Image Crop Tool, Image Rotate Tool, Image Blur Tool and Grayscale Image Converter — all run inside the browser, which means your photo is never uploaded anywhere. Image Background Remover strips the background, Image Color Picker reads an exact hex value out of any image, and Poster Printer spreads one image across several A4 sheets to make a large poster. Before you publish original work, Image Watermark stamps your name or logo across it — a corner mark for credit, or a tiled pattern that cannot simply be cropped off. One tool worth knowing for privacy reasons: EXIF Metadata Remover strips GPS coordinates and camera details out of a photo before you post it.

Image Blur Tool hides something visually. If it matters that the hidden content cannot be recovered, do not use an image — use Redact PDF instead.

12Generate, hash, convert, conceal

Passwords, keys and privacy

Password Generator creates strong passwords and Password Strength Checker tells you how long an existing one would hold up. Hash Generator MD5/SHA produces MD5 and SHA checksums. If you work on servers, SSH Key Generator creates key pairs, and PEM to PPK Converter and PPK to PEM Converter convert between the OpenSSH and PuTTY formats. For privacy, EXIF Metadata Remover strips location data out of a photo, and Image Steganography hides a message inside an image.

PEM to PPK Converter handles unencrypted RSA keys only and outputs PPK v2. It will not convert Ed25519 or ECDSA keys, which are what ssh-keygen produces by default today.

15Where two tools seem to do the same thing

Telling apart the tools that look alike

Some tools overlap on purpose. Picking the wrong one wastes time or, in a couple of cases, leaves sensitive content recoverable — so here is the difference, stated plainly.

The pairHow they differ
Image Compressor vs Compress Image to Exact KBBoth can target a size in kilobytes. Reach for the Compressor when the job is a batch or a whole folder, or when the input is PNG, GIF, BMP or AVIF. Reach for Compress to Exact KB when a single file has to clear a hard limit on a government or job portal and that is the only requirement.
PDF Extract vs PDF SplitExtract pulls out a chosen subset of pages, and can emit them in the order you clicked. Split divides a whole document by a rule — every page, every N pages, a range, or odd/even. Note that despite its name PDF Extract does not pull out text, and no tool on the site performs OCR on a scanned PDF.
Image Watermark vs Add Watermark to PDFSame idea, different file. Image Watermark marks a raster photo — JPG, PNG, WebP — and gives you an image back. Add Watermark to PDF marks the pages of a document and keeps it a real, still-searchable PDF. Watermarking a PDF by exporting it to images first would throw away its text layer.
Handwriting Tracing Worksheet vs Text to Handwriting ConverterOpposite jobs, easily confused. Text to Handwriting turns typed text into an image that looks handwritten — it is output. The Tracing Worksheet produces a blank practice sheet for a child to write on. One finishes work, the other sets it.
Image Blur Tool vs Redact PDFImage Blur is a visual edit — the content is hidden from view. Redact PDF destroys the underlying text and content. For anything where the hidden material must be unrecoverable rather than merely hard to see, Redact PDF is the correct answer.
PDF Compress vs Flatten PDFCompress reduces file size. Flatten makes form fields and annotations non-editable. Two different jobs, however similar the names sound.
JWT Debugger vs JWT EncoderThe Debugger decodes and verifies an existing token. The Encoder mints a new one.
GST Calculator vs Offline Invoice GeneratorThe calculator is a single-amount GST split — add or extract, CGST+SGST or IGST. The Invoice Generator is a full document: line items, HSN, shipping, round-off, amount in words and a PDF. Use the calculator to check one figure; use the generator to send a bill.
Resume Builder vs ATS Resume CheckerThe Builder writes a new resume and exports a text-based PDF or DOCX. The Checker scores a resume you already have against a job description. Neither uses an LLM.
EMI Calculator vs Home Loan CalculatorThe EMI Calculator is the general loan tool. The Home Loan and Car Loan calculators add domain-specific inputs.

16Cost, privacy, offline use and regional scope

Frequently asked questions

Are all the tools really free?

Yes. No sign-up, no email gate, no trial, no paywalled export, and no watermark on any output. There is no limit on how many times you can use them.

Are my files uploaded anywhere?

Almost every tool runs entirely in your browser — the file is read with the browser's File API, processed in JavaScript or WebAssembly on your own device, and written straight back to your downloads folder. There is no upload step. There are a few exceptions, and they are named explicitly in the next answer.

Which tools do reach the internet?

The ones whose entire purpose is to query the public internet: DNS Lookup, DNS Propagation Checker, SSL Checker, Website SEO Checker, Internet Speed Test and URL Shortener. A few tools also have an optional "import from URL" field. None of these should be described as fully offline or fully private.

Do these tools work offline?

Only the ones that register a service worker: Flatten PDF, Text to Handwriting, Online Notepad, Minesweeper, Poster Printer, Scientific Calculator, Salary Slip Generator, Rent Receipt Generator, Epoch Converter and the Offline Invoice Generator. Everything else needs connectivity on first load because the libraries come from a CDN — but your file still never leaves the device.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no login anywhere on the site. Settings are stored on your own device, never on a server.

Which country are the finance and tax tools built for?

India. EPF, ESI, state-wise professional tax, GST, income-tax slabs under both regimes, PPF, SIP and the lakh/crore number format are all India-first. Wherever a statutory rate applies, the page carries a "last reviewed" date and the rate stays editable.

Can I use the output commercially?

Yes. The output is yours, there is no watermark, and no attribution is required. Verifying generated documents — payslips, rent receipts, invoices, resumes — is your responsibility, as the Terms set out.
