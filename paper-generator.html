Frequently asked questions

Is this graph paper generator free?

Yes — completely free with no sign-up. PDFs are generated as vector graphics entirely in your browser.

How do I print dot grid paper at actual size?

Select your paper size, download the PDF or click Print, then in the printer dialog set Scale to 100% or Actual Size. Use the calibration ruler on page 1 to verify with a physical ruler.

What is dot grid spacing for Bullet Journal?

The standard Bullet Journal dot grid uses 5 mm spacing between dots. ToolAdda includes a 5 mm preset on A4 and Letter paper.

What is four-line handwriting paper?

Four-line paper has a top line, dashed midline, baseline, and descender line to guide English handwriting. Indian school notebooks often use blue solid and red dashed lines.

Can I generate multi-page PDFs?

Yes — choose 1 to 100 pages. Each page can include page numbers and optional header fields.

What is the 5 mm Bullet Journal preset?

It creates a dot grid with 5 mm spacing between dots — the standard Bullet Journal layout on A4 or Letter paper.

Can I make lined paper with a red margin?

Yes — choose Lined paper, pick wide/college/narrow rule, and enable the red left margin and optional header row.

Does music manuscript include TAB and chord boxes?

Yes — toggle treble clef, 6-line TAB staff, and fretboard chord boxes on the Music tab.

Can I generate Hindi handwriting practice sheets?

Yes — use Four-Line (Hindi three-line with shirorekha) or Hindi tab for traceable Swar/Vyanjan practice.
